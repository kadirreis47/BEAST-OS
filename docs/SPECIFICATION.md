# BEAST OS — Product Specification

Version: 0.1.0  
Status: Active  
Owner: Product Team

## 1. Purpose

BEAST OS is a modular life-management system for men. It combines health, physical training, nutrition, psychology, relationships, money, career, communication, lifestyle and practical tools in one clear structure.

It is not designed to impress the reader with information. It is designed to help the reader make better decisions and take useful action.

## 2. Mission

Help men build:

- a healthier and stronger body,
- a calmer and more capable mind,
- better habits and self-control,
- healthier relationships,
- financial awareness,
- professional competence,
- and a structured life.

## 3. Product promise

The user should be able to open BEAST OS with a real problem and quickly find:

1. what matters most,
2. what to do next,
3. what to avoid,
4. how to track progress,
5. when professional help is appropriate.

## 4. Audience

Primary audience: men aged 18–45 who want a structured, practical and evidence-informed system.

The content must remain useful to beginners while offering deeper layers for experienced users.

## 6. Content layers

Every major topic should use three levels:

- **Start:** minimum effective action.
- **Practice:** repeatable system and protocol.
- **Deep dive:** mechanisms, nuances and limitations.

## 7. Evidence labels

- **A — Strong:** guidelines, systematic reviews, meta-analyses or multiple high-quality trials.
- **B — Moderate:** several consistent studies or one strong trial.
- **C — Limited:** early or mixed research.
- **D — Practice-based:** professional consensus or field practice with limited direct evidence.
- **E — Opinion:** interpretation, preference or editorial judgment.

Health, medicine, finance and legal claims must always state limitations and professional-help thresholds.

## 8. Required chapter structure

Each core chapter must include:

1. Purpose
2. Key takeaway
3. Why it matters
4. Evidence level
5. Practical protocol
6. Examples
7. Common mistakes
8. Decision rules
9. Checklist
10. When to seek professional help
11. References or source plan
12. Revision history

## 9. Quality requirements

A chapter is publishable only when it is:

- clear,
- actionable,
- internally consistent,
- responsibly worded,
- easy to scan,
- free of unnecessary repetition,
- and structured for web, EPUB and PDF output.

## 10. Safety boundaries

BEAST OS must not:

- diagnose disease,
- promise guaranteed outcomes,
- encourage unsafe weight loss,
- recommend prescription drugs without clinician involvement,
- treat manipulative relationship behavior as strength,
- present speculation as fact,
- or give personalized financial instructions without risk context.

## 11. Product outputs

The same content base should support:

- static website,
- EPUB,
- PDF,
- calculators,
- trackers,
- and future mobile applications.

## 12. Success criteria

BEAST OS succeeds when users can say:

- “I know what to do today.”
- “I understand why it matters.”
- “I can measure whether it is working.”
- “I know when to adjust or seek help.”
