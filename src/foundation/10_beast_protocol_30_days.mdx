# BEAST Protocol

> Do not chase perfection.
>
> Build momentum.

---

# Mission

For the next 30 days your only objective is consistency.

Forget motivation.

Forget perfection.

Complete today's checklist.

---

# Daily Rules

Every day:

- Sleep 7.5–9 hours.
- Drink 2.5–3.5 L water.
- Eat enough protein.
- Train or walk.
- Read for 20 minutes.
- Stretch 5–10 minutes.
- Prepare tomorrow.

---

# Week 1

## Objective

Build routine.

No intensity.

Just consistency.

Daily Goals

- Wake at the same time.
- Sleep at the same time.
- Walk every day.
- Drink enough water.

---

# Week 2

## Objective

Add strength training.

Continue Week 1.

Add:

- 3 resistance workouts.
- Protein target.
- Daily planning.

---

# Week 3

## Objective

Improve focus.

Continue previous weeks.

Add:

- 60 minutes Deep Work.
- No phone during meals.
- Morning planning.

---

# Week 4

## Objective

Optimization.

Review everything.

Improve weak areas.

Measure progress.
