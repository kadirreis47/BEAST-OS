# Habit Engine

> You do not rise to your goals. You fall to your systems.

---

# Overview

Habits are repeated behaviors that require less conscious effort over time.

The purpose of the Habit Engine is to make healthy actions automatic.

---

# The Habit Loop

Cue

↓

Action

↓

Reward

↓

Repeat

Every habit follows this cycle.

Change the cue or the reward and the habit becomes easier to build or easier to break.

---

# Build Good Habits

Make it:

- Obvious
- Easy
- Attractive
- Repeatable

---

# Break Bad Habits

Make it:

- Invisible
- Difficult
- Unrewarding

---

# Habit Stacking

Attach a new habit to an existing one.

Examples:

After brushing teeth

↓

Drink water.

After breakfast

↓

Take vitamins.

After training

↓

Stretch.

After dinner

↓

Prepare tomorrow.

---

# Environment Design

The environment should make good choices easier.

Examples:

Fruit visible.

Water bottle on desk.

Workout clothes prepared.

Phone charger outside bedroom.

---

# Never Miss Twice

Missing one day is normal.

Missing two days starts a new habit.

Rule:

Miss once.

Recover immediately.

---

# Daily Minimum

Even on your worst day:

- Walk 10 minutes.
- Drink water.
- Eat one protein-rich meal.

Keep the habit alive.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Depending on motivation.

❌ Changing too many habits at once.

❌ Expecting perfection.

❌ Giving up after one bad day.
