# Sleep Protocol

> Sleep is the foundation of recovery, hormone regulation, learning, and long-term health.

---

# Overview

A high-quality sleep routine is one of the highest return habits in BEAST OS.

Without consistent sleep:

- Training quality decreases.
- Recovery slows.
- Appetite regulation becomes more difficult.
- Mental performance declines.
- Mood becomes less stable.

The goal is not to sleep "more".

The goal is to sleep **consistently**.

---

# Primary Objective

Build a stable circadian rhythm.

Consistency matters more than occasionally sleeping for very long periods.

---

# Beast Sleep Targets

| Metric | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Sleep Duration | 7.5–9 hours |
| Bed Time | Same every day |
| Wake Time | Same every day |
| Screen-Free Time | 30–60 min before sleep |
| Room Temperature | 17–20°C (comfortable, cool environment) |
| Room Light | Dark |

---

# Beast Evening Protocol

## T -60 Minutes

- Stop unnecessary screen use.
- Reduce bright light.
- Prepare tomorrow's clothes and schedule.

---

## T -30 Minutes

- Read a physical book.
- Gentle stretching (5–10 min).
- Avoid heavy meals.

---

## T -10 Minutes

- Slow breathing.
- Relax.
- No phone.

---

# Morning Protocol

Within the first 30 minutes:

- Drink water.
- Open curtains.
- Get natural daylight if possible.
- Move your body for 5 minutes.

---

# Caffeine Rules

Recommended:

Morning to early afternoon.

Avoid caffeine approximately 8 hours before your planned bedtime if it affects your sleep.

---

# Environment

Ideal bedroom:

- Dark
- Quiet
- Cool
- Clean
- Used primarily for sleep

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Different sleep schedule every weekend.

❌ Using the phone in bed.

❌ Drinking large amounts of caffeine late in the day.

❌ Eating a very heavy meal immediately before bed.

❌ Using alcohol as a sleep aid.

---

- [ ] Same wake time
- [ ] Screen-free before sleep

- [ ] Morning daylight
- [ ] Water after waking

Target:

6/6

---

# Weekly Review

Questions:

Did I sleep at roughly the same time every day?

Did I wake without excessive snoozing?

Did I feel rested during training?

What disturbed my sleep this week?

---

# Key Takeaways

- Consistency is more important than perfection.
- Recovery starts before you fall asleep.
- Better sleep improves every other system inside BEAST OS.
