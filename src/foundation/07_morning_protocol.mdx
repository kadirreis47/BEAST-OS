# Morning Protocol

> Win the first hour. The rest of the day becomes easier.

---

# First 60 Minutes

## Minute 0

Wake up.

No snooze.

---

## Minute 1

Make your bed.

---

## Minute 5

Drink 500–750 ml water.

---

## Minute 10

Open the curtains.

Get natural daylight.

---

## Minute 15

Bathroom and hygiene.

---

## Minute 25

Light mobility.

5–10 minutes.

---

## Minute 35

Healthy breakfast.

High protein.

---

## Minute 50

Review today's priorities.

Choose the three most important tasks.

---

# Morning Rules

No social media.

No unnecessary notifications.

No doomscrolling.

No lying in bed awake.

---

# Beast Checklist

- [ ] Bed made
- [ ] Water consumed
- [ ] Daylight received
- [ ] Mobility completed
- [ ] Breakfast completed
- [ ] Daily plan written

---

# Key Takeaways

The first hour programs the rest of the day.
