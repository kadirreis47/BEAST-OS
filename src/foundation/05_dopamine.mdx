# Dopamine Management

> Control your dopamine, or your dopamine will control you.

---

# Overview

Dopamine is not the "pleasure hormone."

It is primarily involved in motivation, anticipation and learning.

The problem today is not dopamine itself.

The problem is constant overstimulation.

---

# Modern Dopamine Traps

Examples:

- Endless social media scrolling
- Pornography
- Constant notifications
- Gambling
- Junk food
- Short-form videos
- Endless gaming

These activities provide frequent rewards with little effort.

---

# High Value Dopamine

Build dopamine through effort.

Examples:

★★★★★ Training

★★★★★ Learning

★★★★★ Building projects

★★★★★ Reading

★★★★★ Walking

★★★★☆ Cooking

★★★★☆ Meditation

---

# Low Value Dopamine

Limit:

❌ Doomscrolling

❌ Porn

❌ Random YouTube loops

❌ Constant phone checking

❌ Sugary binge eating

---

# Dopamine Reset

When feeling mentally exhausted:

1. Leave the phone.

2. Walk outside.

3. Drink water.

4. Breathe deeply.

5. Return to one meaningful task.

---

# Environment Rules

Remove temptation.

Examples:

Delete distracting apps.

Disable unnecessary notifications.

Keep books visible.

Keep the phone away while working.

---

# Focus Block

Recommended:

50 minutes focused work

↓

10 minutes break

↓

Repeat

During focus blocks:

No phone.

No social media.

One task only.

# Common Mistakes

❌ Trying to quit every bad habit at once.

❌ Depending on motivation.

❌ Constant multitasking.

❌ Rewarding yourself before completing the task.
