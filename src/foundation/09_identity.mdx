# Identity First

> Every action is a vote for the person you want to become.

---

# Overview

Goals create direction.

Identity creates consistency.

Do not ask:

"What do I want to achieve?"

Ask:

"Who do I want to become?"

---

# Identity Pyramid

Identity

↓

Beliefs

↓

Standards

↓

Habits

↓

Actions

↓

Results

Results are the consequence.

Identity is the cause.

---

# Beast Identity

Examples:

"I am someone who trains."

"I am someone who keeps promises."

"I am someone who takes care of my health."

Not:

"I hope I become disciplined."

---

# Raise Your Standards

Standards create behavior.

Behavior creates lifestyle.

Lifestyle creates identity.

---

# Daily Identity Questions

Did I act like the person I want to become today?

Did today's choices match my standards?

What one decision made me stronger?

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Chasing motivation.

❌ Depending only on goals.

❌ Defining yourself by one bad day.

---

# Beast Checklist

- [ ] I kept my word today.
- [ ] I protected my standards.
- [ ] I acted intentionally.

---

# Key Takeaways

Temporary motivation creates temporary results.

Strong identity creates permanent habits.
