# Environment Design

> Your environment is stronger than your motivation.

---

# Overview

Most people try to change themselves.

BEAST OS changes the environment first.

Your surroundings silently influence your daily decisions.

Good environments make good decisions easier.

---

# The Golden Rule

Never rely on willpower if your environment can solve the problem.

---

# Remove Friction

Increase the ease of good habits.

Examples:

- Fill your water bottle before bed.
- Prepare tomorrow's clothes.
- Keep healthy food visible.
- Leave workout equipment ready.

---

# Add Friction

Make bad habits harder.

Examples:

- Remove social media from the home screen.
- Keep snacks out of sight.
- Charge your phone outside the bedroom.
- Log out of distracting websites.

---

# Environment Audit

Ask yourself:

Does my room encourage productivity?

Does my kitchen encourage healthy eating?

Does my desk encourage focus?

Does my phone encourage distraction?

---

# Digital Environment

Keep only useful apps.

Disable unnecessary notifications.

Organize folders.

Remove visual clutter.

---

# Physical Environment

Bedroom

- Dark
- Cool
- Quiet
- Clean

Workspace

- Minimal
- Bright
- Organized

Training Area

- Equipment visible
- Water available
- Space prepared

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Expecting discipline in a chaotic room.

❌ Keeping temptations within reach.

❌ Working where you relax.

❌ Ignoring digital clutter.
