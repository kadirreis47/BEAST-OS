# Discipline

> Discipline is choosing what matters most over what feels good now.

---

# Overview

Motivation changes every day.

Discipline remains.

The purpose of discipline is to remove negotiation.

You don't ask yourself if you should train.

You simply train.

---

# The Discipline Pyramid

Identity

↓

Values

↓

Systems

↓

Habits

↓

Daily Actions

Never build from the bottom.

Build from identity first.

---

# Discipline Rules

Rule 1

Never negotiate with yourself.

---

Rule 2

---

Rule 3

---

Rule 4

---

Rule 5

Small wins every day.

---

# The 2-Minute Rule

If starting feels difficult,

make the first action take less than two minutes.

Examples:

Put on training shoes.

Fill your water bottle.

Open the book.

Sit at your desk.

Starting creates momentum.

---

# Eliminate Friction

Bad habits become easier when friction is low.

Good habits become easier when friction is removed.

Examples:

Prepare gym clothes tonight.

Keep healthy food visible.

Charge your phone outside the bedroom.

---

# Daily Standards

Every day:

- Sleep on schedule.
- Eat enough protein.
- Move your body.
- Learn something.
- Prepare tomorrow.

No excuses.

---

# Common Discipline Killers

❌ Waiting for motivation.

❌ Perfectionism.

❌ Too many goals.

❌ Poor environment.

❌ No planning.

---

# Recovery Rule

Missed one day?

Return immediately.

Never miss twice.

---

# Decision Tree

Didn't train today?

↓

Injured?

↓

YES

Recovery session.

↓

NO

15-minute minimum workout.

↓

Done.

Consistency maintained.

# Weekly Review

Did I act according to my goals,

or according to my emotions?

Where did I negotiate with myself?

How will I improve next week?

# Key Takeaways

Discipline is not punishment.

Discipline is self-respect.

The disciplined life becomes the easier life.
