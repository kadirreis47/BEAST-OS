# Sleep Cycles

> Sleep quality is determined by structure, not only duration.

---

# Mental Model

Eight hours of fragmented sleep is not equal to eight hours of continuous sleep.

Your brain follows repeating cycles throughout the night.

---

# One Sleep Cycle

Approximate length:

90 minutes

Each cycle contains:

- Light Sleep
- Deep Sleep
- REM Sleep

All three stages matter.

---

# Deep Sleep

Primary roles:

★★★★★ Physical recovery

★★★★★ Tissue repair

★★★★★ Growth hormone release

---

# REM Sleep

Primary roles:

★★★★★ Memory

★★★★★ Learning

★★★★★ Emotional processing

★★★★★ Creativity

---

# What Disrupts Sleep Architecture

❌ Alcohol

❌ Frequent awakenings

❌ Bright light before bed

❌ Irregular sleep schedule

❌ Excess caffeine late in the day

---

# Build Better Sleep

Maintain:

- Consistent bedtime
- Dark room
- Cool room
- Quiet environment
- Morning daylight exposure

---

# Beast Metrics

Track:

- Bedtime consistency
- Wake-up consistency
- Sleep duration
- Morning energy (1–10)

---

# Beast Checklist

- [ ] Same bedtime
- [ ] Same wake-up time
- [ ] Dark bedroom
- [ ] Phone outside bed
- [ ] Morning daylight

---

# Quick Wins

✓ Open curtains within 10 minutes of waking.

✓ Avoid unnecessary screens before bed.

✓ Keep your bedroom cool.

---

# When to Seek Medical Care

Persistent loud snoring, pauses in breathing during sleep, excessive daytime sleepiness despite adequate sleep, or other symptoms suggestive of a sleep disorder should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

---

# Key Takeaways

Good sleep is built through consistent habits, not luck.

Protect your nights.

Your days depend on them.
