# Muscle Basics

> Muscles are built through adaptation, not effort alone.

---

# Mental Model

Training is a signal.

Recovery is the construction phase.

Nutrition provides the building materials.

---

# Main Functions

- Movement
- Joint stability
- Posture
- Heat production
- Glucose storage
- Physical performance

---

---

# Muscle Protein Synthesis

Resistance training increases muscle protein synthesis.

Adequate dietary protein supports this process.

Repeated training over time leads to measurable adaptations.

---

# Progressive Overload

Progress requires gradual increases over time.

Examples:

- More repetitions
- More weight
- Better technique
- More total work

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Changing programs every week.

❌ Training without progression.

❌ Eating too little.

❌ Ignoring sleep.

# When to Seek Medical Care

Persistent muscle weakness, severe pain after minor activity, or unexplained muscle loss should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

# Key Takeaways

Muscle is earned through consistency.

Recovery is part of training.
