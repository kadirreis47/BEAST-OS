# Inflammation

> Inflammation is not your enemy.
>
> Uncontrolled inflammation is.

---

# Mental Model

Inflammation is your body's repair response.

Without inflammation:

- wounds would not heal
- muscles would not adapt
- infections would become dangerous

The goal is balance.

---

# Acute Inflammation

Healthy.

Examples:

- Resistance training
- Small injuries
- Infection recovery

Purpose:

Repair.

Adaptation.

Healing.

---

# Chronic Inflammation

Unhealthy.

May be supported by multiple lifestyle factors.

Examples include:

- Poor sleep
- Smoking
- Excess body fat
- Physical inactivity
- Long-term psychological stress
- Poor dietary patterns

---

# Lifestyle Factors That Support Healthy Recovery

★★★★★ Regular sleep

★★★★★ Strength training

★★★★★ Walking

★★★★★ Fruits and vegetables

★★★★★ Omega-3 rich foods

★★★★☆ Healthy body weight

★★★★☆ Stress management

---

# Lifestyle Factors That May Increase Chronic Inflammation

❌ Smoking

❌ Excess alcohol

❌ Chronic sleep deprivation

❌ Long-term inactivity

❌ Highly processed dietary patterns
