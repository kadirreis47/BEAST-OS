# Human Body Overview

> Your body is not a collection of separate parts.
>
> It is one integrated system.

---

# Core Systems

The human body can be simplified into six major systems.

## Nervous System

Responsible for:

- Movement
- Attention
- Memory
- Decision making
- Coordination

---

## Muscular System

Responsible for:

- Strength
- Movement
- Posture
- Heat production

---

## Skeletal System

Responsible for:

- Protection
- Structure
- Joint movement

---

## Cardiovascular System

Responsible for:

- Oxygen delivery
- Nutrient transport
- Waste removal

---

## Endocrine System

Responsible for hormone production.

Examples:

- Testosterone
- Insulin
- Cortisol
- Growth Hormone

---

## Digestive System

Responsible for:

- Food digestion
- Nutrient absorption
- Energy production

---

# The BEAST Principle

Every healthy habit improves multiple systems.

Example:

Training →

Muscles ↑

Heart ↑

Brain ↑

Hormones ↑

Sleep ↑

Mood ↑

# Common Mistakes

❌ Chasing supplements before fixing sleep.

❌ Training without recovery.

❌ Ignoring cardiovascular health.

❌ Believing muscles alone equal health.
