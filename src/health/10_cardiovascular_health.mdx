# Cardiovascular Health

> A strong heart supports every goal inside BEAST OS.

---

# Mental Model

Your heart delivers oxygen and nutrients to every organ.

Better cardiovascular fitness supports daily life, not only athletic performance.

---

# Benefits

★★★★★ Better endurance

★★★★★ Improved recovery

★★★★★ Better circulation

★★★★★ Higher work capacity

★★★★☆ Lower resting heart rate

---

# Build Cardiovascular Fitness

Examples:

- Walking
- Cycling
- Swimming
- Running
- Rowing

Consistency matters more than intensity.

---

# Daily Goal

Move every day.

Avoid remaining sedentary for long uninterrupted periods.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Only lifting weights.

❌ Never walking.

❌ Ignoring conditioning.

---

# Beast Metrics

Track:

- Resting heart rate
- Daily steps
- Weekly cardio sessions

---

# Beast Checklist

- [ ] Walk completed
- [ ] Cardio completed
- [ ] Hydration completed

---

# When to Seek Medical Care

Chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or palpitations with concerning symptoms require prompt medical evaluation.

---

# Key Takeaways

A healthier heart improves every other system.
