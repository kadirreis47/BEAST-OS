# Recovery

> Training is the stimulus.
>
> Recovery is where progress happens.

---

# Mental Model

You do not grow while training.

You grow while recovering.

---

# Recovery Pyramid

Sleep

↓

Nutrition

↓

Hydration

↓

Stress Management

↓

Active Recovery

↓

Supplements

---

# Signs of Good Recovery

- Stable energy
- Good mood
- Strength maintained
- Quality sleep
- Healthy appetite

---

# Signs of Poor Recovery

- Constant fatigue
- Poor sleep
- Decreased performance
- Irritability
- Persistent soreness

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Training hard every day.

❌ Ignoring sleep.

❌ Eating too little.

❌ Believing soreness equals progress.

---

# Decision Tree

Still sore after 72 hours?

↓

YES

Reduce intensity.

↓

NO

Continue training.
