# Digestive Health

> You are not only what you eat.
>
> You are what you digest and absorb.

---

# Mental Model

Digestion begins before food reaches the stomach.

Sleep, stress, movement and nutrition all influence digestive function.

# Foods That Support Digestive Health

- Vegetables
- Fruit
- Oats
- Legumes
- Yogurt / Kefir (if tolerated)
- Fermented foods

# Common Mistakes

❌ Eating too fast

❌ Very low fiber intake

❌ Constant overeating

❌ Ignoring hydration

❌ Remaining inactive after every meal

# When to Seek Medical Care

Persistent abdominal pain, blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, persistent vomiting, or long-lasting changes in bowel habits require medical evaluation.
