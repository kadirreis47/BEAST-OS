# Hydration

> Water supports every cell in your body.

---

# Mental Model

Do not drink water only when you feel thirsty.

Build a routine.

---

# Why Water Matters

Supports:

- Blood circulation
- Body temperature
- Joint lubrication
- Physical performance
- Cognitive performance

---

# Daily Hydration Strategy

Morning

500–750 ml

↓

Meals

Drink regularly

↓

Training

Replace fluid losses

↓

Evening

Maintain hydration without excessive intake immediately before sleep.

---

# Signs You May Need More Fluids

- Dark urine
- Dry mouth
- Headache
- Fatigue
- Reduced exercise performance

These signs are not specific and can have other causes.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Drinking almost all water at night.

❌ Never carrying a water bottle.

❌ Replacing water entirely with sugary drinks.

---

---

# When to Seek Medical Care

Persistent vomiting, severe diarrhea, confusion, inability to keep fluids down, or signs of severe dehydration require prompt medical evaluation.

---

# Key Takeaways

Hydration is a daily habit, not a last-minute correction.
