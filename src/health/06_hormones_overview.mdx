# Hormones Overview

> Hormones coordinate the body.
>
> They do not work alone.

---

# Mental Model

Hormones are messengers.

They tell organs and tissues what to do.

Health depends on balance,

not on maximizing a single hormone.

---

# Major Hormones

## Testosterone

Supports:

- Muscle maintenance
- Bone health
- Sexual function
- Red blood cell production

---

## Insulin

Supports:

- Blood glucose regulation
- Nutrient storage

---

## Cortisol

Supports:

- Stress response
- Energy availability

Chronically elevated levels may be associated with health problems.

---

## Growth Hormone

Supports:

- Growth
- Tissue repair
- Recovery
