# Pain Basics

> Pain is information.
>
> It is not always damage.

---

# Mental Model

Pain is a protective signal created by the nervous system.

The intensity of pain does not always equal the severity of an injury.

Pain deserves attention—not panic.

---

# Types of Pain

## Acute Pain

Usually appears suddenly.

Examples:

- Sprains
- Cuts
- Acute injuries

Purpose:

Protection.

---

## Chronic Pain

Persists for months.

Often requires a broader assessment than simply treating one body part.

---

# During Training

Normal:

- Muscle fatigue
- Mild soreness

Not Normal:

- Sharp pain
- Joint instability
- Sudden loss of strength
- Pain that rapidly worsens

---

# The Traffic Light System

🟢 Green

Minor discomfort.

Continue while monitoring.

---

🟡 Yellow

Moderate discomfort.

Reduce load.

Improve technique.

Monitor symptoms.

---

🔴 Red

Sharp pain.

Stop.

Assess.

Seek medical evaluation if appropriate.

# Quick Wins

✓ Warm up for 10 minutes.

✓ Never ignore sharp pain.

✓ Record symptoms instead of guessing.

---

# When to Seek Medical Care

Seek prompt medical evaluation for severe pain after trauma, inability to bear weight, major swelling or deformity, numbness, weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, chest pain, or other emergency symptoms.
