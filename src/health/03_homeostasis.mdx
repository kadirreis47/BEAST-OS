# Homeostasis

> The body constantly fights to keep itself in balance.

---

# Mental Model

Your body is always trying to survive before it tries to improve.

Every healthy habit supports this balance.

Every unhealthy habit disrupts it.

---

# What Is Homeostasis?

Homeostasis is the body's ability to maintain a stable internal environment.

Examples:

- Body temperature
- Blood pressure
- Blood sugar
- Hydration
- Blood oxygen
- pH balance

---

# Why It Matters

Training creates stress.

Recovery restores balance.

Adaptation makes you stronger.

No balance.

↓

No recovery.

↓

No progress.

---
