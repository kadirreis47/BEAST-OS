# Breathing Fundamentals

> Better breathing supports better movement, recovery and focus.

---

# Mental Model

Breathing is automatic.

Breathing well is a skill.

---

# Diaphragmatic Breathing

The diaphragm is the primary muscle used for breathing.

Aim for relaxed breathing that expands the lower rib cage and abdomen rather than lifting the shoulders excessively.

---

# Benefits

★★★★★ Better relaxation

★★★★★ Improved focus

★★★★★ Better exercise control

★★★★☆ Stress reduction

---

# Daily Practice

5 minutes.

Slow inhale.

Slow exhale.

Comfortable rhythm.

No strain.

---

# During Strength Training

General guideline:

Brace before lifting.

Exhale during the effort phase when appropriate for the exercise.

(Technique may vary depending on the lift.)

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Holding the breath unnecessarily.

❌ Rapid shallow breathing.

❌ Shoulder-dominant breathing all day.

---

# Decision Tree

Feeling stressed?

↓

Pause.

↓

Slow breathing for 2–5 minutes.

↓

Feeling better?

↓

YES

Continue your task.

↓

NO

Consider taking a longer break or seeking additional support if stress remains overwhelming.

# Quick Wins

✓ Take five slow breaths before every meal.

✓ Practice breathing after training.

✓ Relax your shoulders several times each day.

---

# When to Seek Medical Care

Persistent shortness of breath, chest pain, wheezing, coughing up blood, or breathing difficulty at rest requires prompt medical evaluation.

---

# Key Takeaways

You breathe thousands of times every day.

Improving just a small percentage of those breaths can positively influence your daily life.
