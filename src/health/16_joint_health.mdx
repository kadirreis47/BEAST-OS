# Joint Health

> Healthy joints make lifelong movement possible.

---

# Mental Model

Strong muscles protect joints.

Controlled movement protects cartilage.

Consistency protects both.

---

# Pillars of Joint Health

★★★★★ Resistance training

★★★★★ Healthy body weight

★★★★★ Mobility

★★★★★ Recovery

★★★★☆ Proper technique

---

# Daily Joint Care

Move every day.

Warm up before training.

Use full pain-free range of motion.

Increase loads gradually.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Skipping warm-ups.

❌ Poor lifting technique.

❌ Increasing weight too quickly.

❌ Ignoring pain.

---

# Decision Tree

Pain during exercise?

↓

Sharp pain?

↓

YES

Stop.

Assess.

↓

NO

Reduce load.

Review technique.

# Quick Wins

✓ Spend five minutes warming up.

✓ Move every major joint daily.

✓ Don't rush weight increases.

---

# When to Seek Medical Care

Joint swelling, locking, instability, deformity, or persistent pain should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.
