# Immune System

> Your immune system is trained every day by your lifestyle.

---

# Mental Model

A healthy immune system is not "stronger".

It is better regulated.

The goal is balance.

---

# Primary Functions

- Fight infections
- Repair damaged tissue
- Remove abnormal cells
- Support recovery

---

# Daily Habits That Support Immune Function

★★★★★ Consistent sleep

★★★★★ Regular exercise

★★★★★ Adequate protein

★★★★★ Fruits and vegetables

★★★★★ Good hygiene

★★★★☆ Stress management

---

# Habits That Can Reduce Recovery Capacity

❌ Smoking

❌ Chronic sleep deprivation

❌ Long-term inactivity

❌ Excess alcohol

❌ Persistent psychological stress

# When to Seek Medical Care

High fever, persistent fever, repeated unexplained infections, significant weight loss, or other concerning symptoms should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

# Key Takeaways

Your immune system reflects your daily habits more than isolated actions.

Small healthy decisions accumulate.
