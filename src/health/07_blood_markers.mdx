# Blood Markers Overview

> Numbers do not define your health.
> Trends are more valuable than a single result.

---

# Mental Model

A blood test is a snapshot.

Repeated measurements under similar conditions provide a clearer picture.

---

# Core Blood Markers

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Shows information about:

- Red blood cells
- White blood cells
- Platelets

---

## Fasting Blood Glucose

Used to evaluate blood sugar regulation.

---

## HbA1c

Reflects average blood glucose over approximately the previous 2–3 months.

---

## Lipid Panel

Includes:

- Total Cholesterol
- LDL
- HDL
- Triglycerides

---

## Liver Function

Common examples:

- ALT
- AST

---

## Kidney Function

Common examples:

- Creatinine
- eGFR

---

## Iron Status

Examples:

- Ferritin
- Serum Iron

---

## Vitamin Status

Commonly requested when clinically appropriate:

- Vitamin D
- Vitamin B12
- Folate

---

# Tracking Rule

Always compare:

Same laboratory

↓

Similar time of day

↓

Similar fasting status

↓

Similar conditions

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Comparing unrelated tests.

❌ Looking at only one value.

❌ Ignoring symptoms.

❌ Self-diagnosing from internet searches.

# When to Seek Medical Care

Persistent fatigue, unexplained weight loss, chest pain, fainting, blood in stool or urine, or any markedly abnormal test result should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional.

---

# Key Takeaways

Blood tests support decisions.

They do not replace medical evaluation.
