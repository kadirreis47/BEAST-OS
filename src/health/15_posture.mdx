# Posture

> Good posture is efficient posture.

---

# Mental Model

There is no perfect posture.

The best posture is the one you can change regularly.

Movement is healthier than staying in one position all day.

---

# Benefits

★★★★★ Better movement

★★★★★ Better breathing

★★★★★ Better lifting mechanics

★★★★☆ Less stiffness

★★★★☆ Better body awareness

---

# Daily Rules

Every 45–60 minutes:

Stand up.

Walk for 2–5 minutes.

Move your shoulders.

Move your hips.

Reset your posture.

---

# Desk Setup

Monitor:

Eye level.

Keyboard:

Relaxed shoulders.

Feet:

Flat on the floor.

Back:

Supported.

---

# Smartphone Rule

Hold the phone higher.

Avoid looking down continuously.

Take frequent breaks.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Sitting for hours.

❌ Rounded shoulders all day.

❌ Never moving the thoracic spine.

❌ Ignoring hip mobility.

---

# Decision Tree
